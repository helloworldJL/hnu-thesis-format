# hnu-thesis-format v1.0.0

## English

`v1.0.0` is the first public release of **hnu-thesis-format**, a privacy-conscious local helper for the Hunan University academic doctoral dissertation workflow.

### Release contents

- A Codex skill scoped to `academic_phd`, with an explicit rule hierarchy and manual-review boundaries.
- Local DOCX validation and conservative formatting command surfaces, plus redacted JSON and HTML review reports by default.
- A private UTF-8 `--title-file` input for the formatter, avoiding a real dissertation title in a command line.
- Document-package safety checks; formatting for recognised paragraph types and positively recognised scholarly tables; preservation of existing headers and complex/uncertain content for manual review.
- English and Simplified Chinese installation, usage, report-interpretation, privacy-boundary, rule-source, limitation, and release guidance, plus English community, security, citation, and licensing files.
- Synthetic-only public examples and an explicit statement that official PDFs, templates, and example documents are not redistributed.

### Important boundaries

- This is an independent project, not an official Hunan University template, policy interpretation, or approval tool.
- It is not affiliated with, authorized by, sponsored by, or endorsed by Hunan University.
- A clean automated report is not proof of submission readiness. Verify the current official and programme requirements, inspect the rendered Word document, and complete all human review steps.
- The 2025 HNU source cites GB/T 7714-2015, while the [national registry marks that edition abolished](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=7FA63E9BBA56E60471AEDAEBDE44B14C) and lists [GB/T 7714-2025 as current from 2026-07-01](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=C6CE52E55AC09B9C79A20AEA77CEDD14). The tool does not rewrite bibliographic content and does not claim comprehensive GB/T 7714 compliance; verify the current HNU/college rule before submission.
- Quantity and unit semantics remain manual review. The HNU source’s 1986 GB 3100–3102 references have 1993 successors listed as current by the national registry; this release does not certify national-standard compliance.
- Default reports omit input paths and document excerpts. `--include-sensitive-details` adds short excerpts for private review only. Keep your original thesis and every generated artifact private unless it has been reviewed for the intended audience.

## 中文

`v1.0.0` 是 **hnu-thesis-format** 的首个公开发布版本。该项目面向湖南大学学术学位博士论文流程，强调本地处理、隐私保护与人工复核边界。

### 发布内容

- 面向 `academic_phd` 的 Codex skill，明确规则层级与人工复核边界。
- 本地 DOCX 校验与保守格式化命令，以及默认已脱敏的 JSON、HTML 审核报告。
- 格式化器支持私密 UTF-8 `--title-file` 输入，避免将真实论文题名写入命令行。
- 文档包安全检查；仅处理可识别段落类型和被明确识别的学术表格；既有页眉与复杂/歧义内容保留给人工判断。
- 中英文安装、使用、报告解读、隐私边界、规则来源、限制与发布说明，以及英文社区协作、安全、引用和许可证文件。
- 所有公开示例均为合成内容，并明确不重新分发官方 PDF、模板或示范文件。

### 重要边界

- 本项目是独立工具，不是湖南大学官方模板、政策解释或审批工具。
- 本项目与湖南大学不存在隶属、授权、赞助或背书关系。
- 自动化报告无误并不等于可以提交。请对照当期官方与学院要求，检查 Word 最终渲染，并完成全部人工审核环节。
- HNU 2025 年来源引用 GB/T 7714-2015，但[国家标准平台显示该版本已废止](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=7FA63E9BBA56E60471AEDAEBDE44B14C)，并列示 [GB/T 7714-2025 自 2026-07-01 起现行](https://openstd.samr.gov.cn/bzgk/std/newGbInfo?hcno=C6CE52E55AC09B9C79A20AEA77CEDD14)。本工具不改写参考文献内容，也不声称全面符合 GB/T 7714；提交前应核验最新 HNU/学院口径。
- 量和单位语义仍属于人工复核范围。HNU 来源对 1986 年 GB 3100–3102 的引用已有国家标准平台列示的 1993 年替代标准；本版本不认证国家标准符合性。
- 默认报告不含输入路径和论文摘录。`--include-sensitive-details` 仅为私密复核加入短摘录；原始论文和任何生成产物在面向预定读者完成检查前均应保持私密。
