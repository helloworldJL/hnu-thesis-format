## Known conflict

The Sample PDF p. 9 calls out 3–8 keywords; Rule PDF p. 3, §2.2.2 requires 3–6.
Use 3–6. Do not reproduce source-example names, titles, identification details,
adviser details, affiliations, or committee records.

The Sample PDF pp. 10 and 17 display the university label on even pages; Rule
PDF p. 19, §4.3 requires the thesis title. The Sample’s committee list is not a
three-line-table automation target, and its degree/attachment labels are
annotations or placeholders rather than final dissertation content.

The Rule PDF p. 18, §4.3 literally specifies a “15 pt” thick-thin header rule;
the supplied rendered Sample pp. 9–10 visually corresponds to about 1.5 pt upper
and 0.75 pt lower strokes. This is an apparent source ambiguity or likely decimal
issue. Preserve the Rule wording, use the sample only to guide a rendered check,
and confirm against the current official template rather than silently changing
the source requirement.
