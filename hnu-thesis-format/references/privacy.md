# Privacy and public-artifact policy

Treat thesis drafts, source samples, and rendered outputs as potentially sensitive.
The public skill must contain no personal identifiers, student numbers, adviser or
committee details, real thesis titles, signatures, scanned documents, private
paths, or copied sample data.

- Use neutral placeholders such as `<作者姓名>` and `<论文题名>` in instructions,
  tests, screenshots, and examples.
- Keep source PDFs outside the skill package. Store only concise rule summaries,
  page locators, and public official URLs.
- Do not quote, transcribe, or modify the official declaration/authorization text
  in a public artifact. Obtain the current authorized text from HNU when preparing
  a private dissertation.
- Do not include, upload, or expose handwriting, signatures, security
  classifications, defense data, unpublished research, or contact information.
- Before sharing a transformed document or screenshot, inspect headers, fields,
  comments, revision history, metadata, filenames, embedded objects, and PDF text
  for identifiers. Redact or withhold it if any remain.
- If a request asks to use sample personal data or a signature, refuse that reuse
  and substitute a neutral placeholder.
